1. Introduction

Food Runs Counter helps restaurants track server runs during lunch and dinner, manage rosters, and motivate teams with goals, boosts, achievements, and analytics. It also supports guest feedback and monthly NPS reports. This manual explains how to use every feature step by step.

Who should read this?
  • Managers and shift leads who set rosters, start/close shifts, and review performance
  • Servers who interact with counters, achievements, and feedback
  • Owners/analysts who review analytics, NPS, and exports

2. Quick Start

  1. Add or confirm your servers in Admin/Settings.
  2. Set business hours and weekly schedule.
  3. Assign today’s lunch and dinner roster.
  4. Start the shift on the Home/Start screens.
  5. Tap a server’s tile to log runs.
  6. During the transition window: both lunch and dinner may be active.
  7. End of day: close the shift and review history/analytics.
  8. Optional: enter guest feedback (NPS), check monthly reports, export backups.

3. Navigation Basics

Use the app bar or menu buttons to switch between screens:

  • Home: Live counters, roster, goals, boost status
  • Roster / Start Shift / Update Roster: Who’s working lunch vs dinner today
  • Analytics / Advanced Analytics / Station Analytics
  • NPS: Feedback entry, trends, monthly reports
  • Integrity: Data integrity and fairness tools
  • Backup / Export / Data Entry
  • Admin / Settings: Protected or advanced options
  • About / App Features

4. Home Screen

  • Business Status: Shows if the restaurant is open.
  • Working Roster: Active servers based on current time (lunch/transition/dinner).
  • Server Tiles: Tap to log a run; see counts, XP, and streaks.
  • Team Goal and Total: Dynamic target and progress.
  • Boost Mode: XP multiplier when active.
  • Notifications: Milestones, NPS events, and alerts.
Tip
If a tap doesn’t register, ensure the server is in the working roster and the business is currently open.

5. Roster Management

Assign Today’s Roster

  • Select lunch and dinner servers; save the plan.
  • The Home Screen will show who is active right now.

Update Roster (During Shift)

  • Add/remove servers from the working set.
  • During transition, lunch ∪ dinner can be active; after, dinner-only.
  • Choose whether to preserve existing counts for continuing servers.

Archived Servers

  • Archive former employees while keeping their history.

6. Starting and Managing Shifts

  • Start Shift: Confirms roster and starts Lunch or Dinner based on time. Manual override available near the transition.
  • Transition Window: Lunch winds down, dinner begins; both may be active until the window ends.
  • Closing: End-of-day finalizes the shift and clears live counters.
Note
Business hours (including overnight closing) determine when the app considers the restaurant open vs closed.

7. Counting Runs and Special Items

  • Tap a server to log a run; team totals update immediately.
  • Special items (e.g., desserts) can be logged separately if enabled.
  • Streaks build with consecutive taps and can unlock badges.
  • Tap integrity groups per-minute activity and flags anomalies.

8. Boost Mode and XP

  • Boost temporarily multiplies XP awarded per run.
  • Base XP is awarded on each run; achievements add more.
  • Use boost for peak windows or competitions.

9. Badges and Milestones

  • Earn achievements by reaching conditions (streaks, totals, timing).
  • Milestones are larger accomplishments with bonus XP and history.
  • Configure gamification and encouragement in Settings/Admin.

10. Analytics and Dashboards

  • Analytics Overview: Totals, trends, comparisons.
  • Advanced Analytics: Rich charts (line, bar, pie, heatmaps).
  • Station Analytics: By station/section if used.
  • Real-Time Monitor: Live activity view.
  • Trends: Identify improvement areas by period.
Tip
Use date filters to zoom into a specific day, week, or month.

11. NPS Feedback and Monthly Reports

Enter Feedback

  • Record Yes / Maybe / No, plus optional sales, table, guests, and notes.

Monthly Reports

  • View All-Time, 3-Month, and 1-Month NPS percentages and counts.
  • Trend indicators: Improving, Stable, Declining.
  • Data quality improves with more complete feedback entries.

12. Integrity and Compliance

  • Integrity tools detect unusual click patterns and mechanical behavior.
  • Use findings to coach teams and keep competition fair.

13. Smart Scheduling and Recommendations

  • Smart scheduling suggests staffing mixes based on history.
  • Predictive scheduling forecasts volume by shift.
  • Shift optimizer helps place the right people at the right times.

14. Data Management (Backup, Export, Import)

  • Backups: Create and restore application data snapshots.
  • Exports: CSV/JSON for outside analysis or archiving.
  • Imports: Business or bulk data entry when needed.
Security
Backups may contain sensitive performance/guest-related data. Store them securely.

15. Admin and Settings

  • Admin PIN: Required for protected actions (e.g., deleting a shift).
  • Business Hours: Set open/close per day; configure overnight closing.
  • Roster Defaults: Lunch/dinner plans and transition timing.
  • Gamification & Notifications: Enable/disable features and thresholds.
  • About / App Features: Version info and overview.

16. User Profiles, Avatars, and Themes

  • Each server accumulates XP, levels, badges, and milestones.
  • Choose avatars, banners, and wallpapers from built-in assets.
  • Use high-contrast or simpler themes where available for readability.

17. Tips, Shortcuts, and Edge Cases

  • If tapping doesn’t register: ensure the server is active and the business is open now.
  • Transition window: lunch and dinner can both be active; afterward, dinner only.
  • Overnight hours: set “close day” offset so business day spans correctly.
  • Use boost during peak windows or competitions.

18. Troubleshooting

  • Shows Closed but we’re open: Check business hours and AM/PM/overnight configuration.
  • Server tile missing: Ensure they’re on today’s roster for the current period.
  • Shift closes unexpectedly: Confirm the time is within business hours and today’s plan exists; check device time.
  • Can’t delete a shift: Requires the admin PIN.

19. FAQ

  • Can I log special items separately? Yes—use the special item flow if enabled.
  • Do analytics include NPS? Yes—use NPS screens for entry and monthly summaries.
  • How do I restore data? Use Backup Manager to restore from a prior backup.
  • How do we keep competition fair? Monitor integrity tools and set clear tapping rules.
